How to Play

  1. Create a room and share the code, or join a friend's room.
  2. When it's your turn, a group of letters appears (like STR).
  3. Type a real word that contains those letters (like STRING) before the bomb explodes.
  4. Words can't be repeated until someone loses a life.
  5. The bomb gets faster each turn. If the timer runs and the bomb explodes, you lose a heart.
  6. Lose all your hearts and you spectate. Last player standing wins!
